# Spellstorm art — permission

The 102 `ss_*.jpg` files in this directory are the card faces of **Spellstorm**,
© Keith Burgun. Artwork by Christina Zhong, except `ss_rainoftoads.jpg` by Peter
Siecienski. They were extracted from the print-and-play PDFs for the ravel implementation in `game/games/spellstorm.json`.

**Keith Burgun has given written permission for their use.** 

The permission is what these files rest on. They do **not** inherit the
public-domain and Creative Commons terms that cover the rest of the art in this
directory, and it does not extend to anyone who takes them out of this project —
reuse elsewhere needs its own permission from Keith.
