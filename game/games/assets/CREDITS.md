## Art that is not here

**Codex draws its 329 card faces over the network and holds none of them.**
`game/games/codex.json` names two URLs per card, both pointing at
codexcarddb.com's own image host — `codexcards-assets.surge.sh` directly, and
the Cloudinary resizer that site fetches through, which is the one that answers
with CORS headers and so is the one the browser build can use. The list is in
`ideas/codex/card_art.md`.

Codex is © Sirlin Games and the art is theirs. Nothing of it is copied into this
repository, and a player who is offline or whose host is down sees the generated
shape the card would have had anyway. Take the URLs out and the game still plays.

## Ghost Stories

The 64 `gs_*.jpg` files are public-domain Chinese and Japanese paintings from
Wikimedia Commons, downsized to 300px wide (400 for the village tiles) and
otherwise unaltered. They were picked as **four coherent sets** rather than card
by card — forty-one named ghosts cannot be matched one to one against a Ming
scroll — so each set is dealt out in the order the cards are printed, and
`tools/make_ghost_stories.py` says so where it does it:

- **the ghosts** — the Water-Land ritual paintings of Baoning Temple (Ming
  dynasty, Shanxi Museum) and other shuilu scrolls: hosts of solitary souls,
  wrathful kings, and the dead of fire, famine and war.
- **the ten incarnations of Wu-Feng** — paintings of the Ten Kings of Hell.
- **the four Taoists** — Zhong Kui the demon-queller, in Chinese and Japanese
  hands, Shōki being the same figure.
- **the nine village tiles** — details of the town in *Along the River During
  the Qingming Festival*, each chosen for the nearest scene.
