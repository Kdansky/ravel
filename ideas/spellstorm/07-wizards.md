# Spellstorm — The Wizards

Images: `images/large/` (character cards, 1125×1725), `images/wizards/` (2 Wizard Spell Cards each),
`images/traps/` (Omar), `images/oren-potions/` (Oren).

Wizard Spell Cards have no Tier icon; instead they carry a red "**cannot be VOIDed**" mark at bottom right.
Both shuffle into your 8-card starting deck.

Character card layout: name, epithet, **GAME START** / **BATTLE START** / **PASSIVE** blocks, the **ULTIMATE**
(mana cost in a pentagon, name, effect), starting **health** in a heart, and **initiative rating** by a bolt.
Lowest initiative rating starts with the Initiative Tracker.

## Summary

The initiative ratings are all distinct (1–8) — they are a strict turn order across the whole roster, not a
per-matchup tiebreak. Health scales inversely with how disruptive the wizard is: Omar 10, Croh 20.

## Abragail (Water, Earth)

Professor of Magical Chemistry at Azura Academy, Oren's teacher. Levels up across the game via her Journal.

- **ULTIMATE (4) — Level Up!** Add a Research Token to an ability on Abragail's Research Journal.
- Health **16** · Initiative **7** · *"The magic of the Spellstorm is \*fascinating\*! Reading about it doesn't do it justice. Ya gotta get out there and see stuff like this in person."*

| Spell card | Element | Text |

## Croh Vosh (Fire, Water)

Undead Lich, 1000 years asleep at the bottom of the lake, back for revenge. Huge health pool, cannot heal.

- **GAME START — Looming.** Place your 4 DOOM Tokens within reach.
- **PASSIVE — Accursed.** Whenever you would normally heal damage, ignore all healing and **give 1 CURSE** instead.
- **ULTIMATE (6) — DOOOOOOOOOM!** For each DOOM Token you have, you may redraw a card of your choice from your discard to your hand OR `[DRAW]`. If you have no DOOM Tokens, gain a DOOM Token.
- Health **20** · Initiative **8** · *"I've come… to take back… that which is rightfully mine!"*

| Spell card | Element | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Sinking Strike** | Fire | `[MANA]` `[MANA]`. Deal X damage, where X is your number of DOOM Tokens. If the CURSE pile is empty, gain a DOOM Token. `[ULT]` |
| **Undertow** | Water | Opponents discard a random card. You `[DRAW]`. If you have at least 3 more cards than another player, gain a DOOM Token. `[ULT]` |

Note the trap in Croh's design: DOOM Tokens are gained only from *failure states* — an empty CURSE pile, or
being far ahead on cards — and his healing is converted into an attack. He goes last on initiative (8) with the
most health (20).

## Omar Evans (Fire, Water)

Ninja and wanted eco-terrorist, Zuzuni bug-person. Lowest health, but blocks and traps.

- **GAME START.** Gain Omar's 3 Trap cards. Keep them in a nearby face-down pile.
- **ULTIMATE (4) — Hidden Movement.** You may return a card from your discard to your hand or `[DRAW]`. Play a Trap card face down on this card, replacing any existing one (you can't play the same Trap twice in a row).
- Health **10** · Initiative **1** · *Omar Evans is a high profile wanted enemy of the Azure Order, for his repeated sabotage of their imperial infrastructure.*

| Spell card | Element | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Beetle Buster** | Fire | Discard a `[FIRE]` from your hand to deal 2 damage. — `[ULT]` |
| **Shuriken** | Water | **SPECIAL: this card ALWAYS goes first.** Gain `[INIT]`. `[DRAW]` OR chosen opponent discards their revealed card (it does not resolve) and they `[DRAW]`. `[ULT]`. |

Omar has initiative rating **1** — he begins with the Initiative Tracker in any matchup — and Shuriken breaks
initiative order outright. He is the timing wizard.

### Omar's Trap cards (`images/traps/`)

Kept face-down on his character card; revealed at will after the trigger event, then left face up and inactive
until swapped by the next Ultimate.

| Trap | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Mud Trap** | You may reveal this if you COUNTER with a `[FIRE]` card. | Deal 1 damage. `[MANA]`, gain `[INIT]`. |
| **Ice Bomb** | You may reveal this if you COUNTER with a `[WATER]` card. | Give an ICE. `[MANA]`, `[ULT]`. |
| **Dodge!** | You may reveal this when an opponent is dealing damage to you. | The first 2 points of damage you take this round are negated. |

## Bunny Wizard (Water, Earth)

A Stuffie from Bunny Island; healer and local politician. Starts at the lowest health in the game but is the
only wizard who can exceed their starting health.

- **PASSIVE — Double Stitch.** You can heal beyond your starting health, to a maximum of 10 health.
- **PASSIVE — Triple Stitch!** If you heal when already at 10 health, `[DRAW]` for each point of wasted healing.
- **ULTIMATE (5) — Cast a Magic Trick!** Reveal a non-Wizard card from your hand. Resolve it twice and VOID it. All players heal 1.
- Health **8** (max 10) · Initiative **2** · *"Bunny wants to know if… if anyone knows how to get back to the mainland." — Bunny's Handler*

| Spell card | Element | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Buddy System** | Earth | Tier I players `[POWER]`. You `[POWER]` `[MANA]`. You may resolve a different revealed `[EARTH]` card. — `[ULT]` |
| **Snow Day** | Water | Heal 2. Give an ICE to all opponents without any in their discard. `[ULT]`. |

Bunny converts overheal into card draw, which is Blast Score — his healing is a scoring engine, not just
survival. He also heals and empowers *opponents* (Buddy System, Cast a Magic Trick), which is the flavour of
"plays nice" made mechanical.

## Oren Bark (Fire, Earth)

Magical Chemistry student at Azura Academy under Abragail. Push-your-luck gambler.

- **GAME START — Chemist.** Gain your Chemistry Board, 3 Element Tokens (Fire/Earth/Water) and your Potion Deck. Place each Element Token on the "3" spot of its Element.
- **ULTIMATE (5) — Bottoms up, I guess!** Reshuffle the Potion Deck. Draw Potion cards, doing their effect only if you can pay the Element cost. After each, you may draw another card or end the Ultimate. If you draw a **third** card with a TOXIC icon, after resolving it, end the Ultimate and gain an ASH, CURSE and an ICE. After the Ultimate ends, set your Chemistry Board Element levels to **3**.
- Health **16** · Initiative **6** · *After blowing up the lab at Azura Academy, Oren is required to accompany his professor, Abragail, to the Spellstorm.*

| Spell card | Element | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Potion Gun** | Fire | Deal 1 damage. You may give a non-Wizard card from your hand to an opponent's hand. If you did, gain 2 of the Element matching that card. — `[ULT]` |
| **Unstable Formula** | Earth | `[POWER]` `[MANA]`. Lower any one Element by 2 to raise another by 2. If anyone revealed `[WATER]`, +1 Earth Element. `[ULT]` |

### Chemistry Board (`images/large/oren-chemistry.jpg`)

Three beakers — **Fire**, **Earth**, **Water** — each a track from **0 to 6**. Element Tokens start at 3 and
reset to 3 after every Ultimate.

### Potion Deck — 9 cards (`images/oren-potions/`)

Each potion has an **Element cost** (a coloured diamond with a number): you resolve it only if that element is
at or above the number on your Chemistry Board, and doing so lowers that element by that much. Six of the nine
are **TOXIC**; drawing a third TOXIC ends the Ultimate and gives Oren an ASH, CURSE and ICE.

| Potion | Cost | Effect | TOXIC |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"Haste Potion"** | Fire 1 | If you have `[INIT]`, deal 1 damage. Otherwise, gain `[INIT]`. | no |
| **"I Call It… Purple Stuff."** | Fire 2 | `[POWER]` `[MANA]` `[MANA]` | yes |
| **"A Slight… Explosion"** | Fire 3 | Deal 1 damage. Gain 1 of each element. | yes |
| **"Devil's Breath"** | Earth 1 | `[POWER]`. Give an ASH. | yes |
| **"I Think I Just Drank Gasoline"** | Earth 2 | Take 1 damage. Your next Potion Card's effect happens twice (pay the cost once). | yes |
| **"Dragon Elixir"** | Earth 4 | Resolve the top card of the Dragon Deck. | no |
| **"Frost Bomb"** | Water 2 | Opponents lose 1 mana. Give an ICE. | no |
| **"Storm Juice"** | Water 3 | `[DRAW]` `[POWER]` | no |
| **"Health Soda"** | Water 4 | Heal 2. | yes |

Card text on the TOXIC cards reads "gain an ASH, POISON and ICE" — "POISON" there is a leftover name for
**CURSE**; the rulebook and the character card both say CURSE.

## May Danaris (Fire, Earth)

Hacker, ex-Central Intelligence, presumed dead. Plays other people's cards.

- **GAME START — Energize.** Gain 2 of your 3 Energy Tokens.
- **PASSIVE — Dangerous Download.** At the end of a round, May may lose 1 Energy Token and 1 mana. If she did, she may resolve another player's revealed Tier II card.
- **ULTIMATE (6) — Void Traveler.** Gain 3 Energy Tokens. You may resolve a non-Wizard card from your hand or any card in the VOID. Then place that card on the bottom of the Spellstorm Deck.
- Health **12** · Initiative **4** · *"Those dopes at the Prefex still think I'm dead. They're in for a surprise!"*

| Spell card | Element | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Breach** | Earth | Lose 1 or 2 Energy Tokens. If you did, `[POWER]` that many times. If you still have 2 Energy Tokens, chosen opponent reveals their hand and they discard a card of your choice. — `[ULT]` |
| **Star Shot** | Fire | Discard a card to deal 1 damage. If it was Tier II, deal 1 more damage. `[MANA]` for each Energy Token you have. Lose 2 Energy Tokens. — `[ULT]` |

May's Energy caps at 3 and every use spends it, so her Ultimate is a refill as much as an effect. She is the
only wizard who touches the **VOID** as a resource rather than a graveyard.
