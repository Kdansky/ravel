# Spellstorm — Spell Cards (the Spellstorm Deck)

Images: `images/spells/`. Icon spellings: see `02-icons.md`.
63 Spell Cards in the box = the four 6-card starting decks (3 distinct basic cards, 2 copies each per player)
plus the Spellstorm Deck, which includes the 3 Essence cards.

## Basic Spell Cards — the 6-card starting deck

Every player begins with **2× Magic Dart, 2× Block, 2× Power Gem** (one of each element). No Tier icon.

| Card | Element | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **Magic Dart** | Fire | `[MANA]`. If you have `[INIT]`, deal 1 damage. — `[ULT]` |
| **Block** | Water | Gain `[INIT]`. Heal 1 for every `[FIRE]` card revealed. |
| **Power Gem** | Earth | `[POWER]` `[GAIN]` — If you gained a card, discard it. · `[DISCARD]`: `[POWER]` |

Flavour: Magic Dart — "Fire magic is often banned from use in many parts of the world, due to its unpredictable
nature." Block — "He just needs a little splash, is all he needs." — The Splashmaster. Power Gem — "The golden
gems of the Spell Storm take time to develop their power."

## Design observations

- Fire = damage and Initiative churn. Water = healing, Initiative gain, draw, ICE giving. Earth = Power Tokens
  and Tier climbing, and almost every Earth card carries a discard effect (the card **Sift** says so outright).
- **Initiative is a resource, not just turn order.** Fire cards spend it (`lose [INIT]`) for extra damage;
  Water cards keep gaining it. That tension is the game's core economy alongside mana.
- Discard effects reward *not* playing a card, which competes directly with Blast Score (cards remaining in
  hand at Regroup) — so holding cards is doubly rewarded and the tempo cost is real.
- `[GAIN]` is optional except where the card says MUST (**Amber**).
