## Battle Board

Shared play area, oriented for up to four players around a square:

- **Four Battle Card spots**, one per side, each with an adjacent **Initiative** bolt spot (only one Initiative
  Tracker exists — the four spots are so it can sit on whichever player's side holds it).
- **Weather Deck** and **Weather Discard** slots down the left edge.
- Four stacked **ROUND 1 / 2 / 3 / 4** weather slots in the centre — the four cards of the current battle,
  face-down until their round.
- **Power Tokens** supply (top right) and **Mana Tokens** supply (bottom right).
- The **Elemental Counter Wheel** printed near the centre: Fire → Earth → Water → Fire.

## Spellstorm Board

- **THE STORM CLOUD** — five card slots down the left. Three are marked **E** (the three Essence cards seeded
  at setup) and the bottom one is marked **X**. Robot Boy's *Robo Bounce* and *Upgrade* refer to "the X card in
  the Storm Cloud" — this slot. The rulebook never explains the marking.
- **SPELLSTORM DECK** slot at the bottom left, **SPELLSTORM VOID** at the bottom right.
- **BATTLE END** reminder list, top right: 1. Discard 4 Weather Cards · 2. Discard all `[DISCARD]` cards ·
  3. Discard remaining for Blast Score · 4. Award Gem Shards · 5. Gain a card.
- **SPECIAL CARDS** column, four face-up piles, each printed with **what to do when that pile is empty**:

| Pile | If the pile is empty |
|---|---|
| **DRAGON** | Deal 2 damage and gain 2 Storm Shards. |
| **ASH** | VOID an ASH in your hand or discard pile. Lose 2 Power Tokens. |
| **CURSE** | VOID a CURSE in your hand or discard pile. Take 1 damage. |
| **ICE** | VOID an ICE in your hand or discard pile. Discard 2 cards randomly. |

The empty-pile actions are the same shape as the card each pile holds, so running the supply dry never stalls
the game — it just resolves the junk card's cost directly.

Note the board says "Award **Gem Shards**" and the Dragon slot says "gain 2 **Storm Shards**" — the same thing
under two names. The rulebook and every card say **Storm Shard**.

## Stat Board (one per player)

Four tracks, all on one panel:

- **TIER** — two octagonal slots labelled **II** and **III**. A Power Token placed here is permanent and can
  never be lost. Empty = Tier I.
- **POWER TRACK** — six octagonal slots numbered 1–6. Filling all six and gaining another Power Token bumps
  your Tier: the new token goes to the lowest empty Tier slot and the six are returned to the supply.
- **MANA** — a large pentagon area to pile mana tokens on (no numbered track; just a holding area).
- **Health Track** — a **LOSE** space, then hearts **1** through **20**, on two rows.
- **Storm Shard Track** — **START**, then **1** through **7**, then **WIN** (the 8th shard).

The Storm Shard track literally ends in a space labelled WIN and the health track begins at a space labelled
LOSE, so both victory conditions are readable off the board with no counting.

| Token | Count | Look |

| Power Token | 25 | gold octagon gem |

Punchboard 2 also carries round discs with May Danaris' portrait and a second style of skull disc. These are
presumably the 3 Energy Tokens (May) and 3 Element Tokens (Oren), but they are not labelled on the sheet —
check against the component list in `01-rulebook.md` before relying on it.
