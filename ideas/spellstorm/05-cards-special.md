# Spellstorm — Special Cards

Images: `images/special/`. 23 Special Cards: 6 Ice, 6 Ash, 6 Curse, 5 Dragons.
Ice, Ash and Curse sit face-up in supply piles on the Spellstorm Board; the shuffled Dragon deck sits face-up
in its own slot. All Special Cards share a pink text panel.

## Junk cards — ICE, ASH, CURSE

Six of each. All three say **"This can't be played."** and **"(Gain this to your discard.)"** — a hand made
entirely of these is the "Unplayable Hand" case. They still carry an element for the purpose of COUNTERING
when revealed (Robot Boy reveals them; players never can).

| Card | Element | Discard effect | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ICE** | Water | `[DISCARD]`: −1 to Blast Score (if discarded during the Regroup Phase.) | "Ice cut into a cube shape is kind of like a gem." — Beosnook |
| **ASH** | Earth | `[DISCARD]`: Lose 1 Power Token. | The Spellstorm is a highly volcanic area, often covered in ash. |
| **CURSE** | Fire | `[DISCARD]`: Take 1 damage. | "Curses!" — H.M.T. Holliganger, Distinguished Professor at the Azura Academy School of Archaeology |

The three junk cards line up with the three elements and attack the three currencies: ICE attacks Storm Shard
scoring, ASH attacks Power/Tier climbing, CURSE attacks health. That symmetry is worth keeping.

## Dragons — Tier IV

Gained only by filling the Power Track while already at Tier III. Five cards; the deck is shuffled but face-up,
so the next Dragon is always public information. Every Dragon **grants a Storm Shard outright** except the
Fire Dragon, which trades that for raw damage.

| Card | Element | Text | Flavour |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire Dragon** | Fire | Deal 4 damage to a player. `[MANA]` `[MANA]` — `[ULT]` | No Dragon is more feared than the destructive and terrible Fire Dragon. |
| **Wind Dragon** | Fire | Gain `[INIT]`. Gain a **Storm Shard**. You may resolve up to two cards from your hand. — `[ULT]` | The elusive Storm Dragons were considered to be cryptids until very recently. |
| **Ice Dragon** | Water | Heal 3. Gain 1 **Storm Shard**. Give an ICE. — `[ULT]` | The elusive Storm Dragons were considered to be cryptids until very recently. |
| **Storm Dragon** | Water | Deal 3 damage and gain 1 **Storm Shard**. — `[ULT]` | The elusive Storm Dragons were considered to be cryptids until very recently. |
| **Earth Dragon** | Earth | `[GAIN]` `[GAIN]`. Deal 2 damage. Gain 1 **Storm Shard**. Opponents gain an ASH. — `[ULT]` | The Earth Dragons are known to hoard massive amounts of treasure in caves. |

Every Dragon also carries the `[ULT]` icon, so a Dragon turn can chain into your Ultimate.
